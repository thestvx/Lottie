Wink Theory

Marketing Campaign

The beauty market is crowded with brands following similar visual trends. Wink Theory needed a distinctive identity that could stand out, connect with younger audiences, and communicate its playful personality across digital platforms.

We developed a vibrant visual language built around punchy typography, expressive photography, and eye-catching packaging. A cohesive campaign system brought the brand to life across social media, product visuals, and digital touchpoints.
